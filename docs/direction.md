# Creative direction and research

Concept: one garment, a new commercial possibility. An asymmetric Arabic opening pairs strong typography with a warm rack photograph and a paper stock tag. The mark stays recognizable; restrained gold carries chapter numbers and small details. Teal is used as the immersive middle act, then stone and paper restore breathing room.

RTL is authored directly: start-side navigation and typography, reading-order sections, directional arrows, logical CSS spacing. The mobile hero is recomposed and the sticky desktop journey becomes a linear readable story. Motion is confined to selected reveals, hero parallax, image crossfades, and controls. There is no scroll-jacking, autoplay video, fabricated metric, or product application.

Research consulted 2026-09-09:

- https://motion.dev/docs/react-use-scroll — scroll values and transforms without frame-by-frame whole-page React state.
- https://www.motion.dev/docs/react-motion-config — user reduced-motion policy, plus explicit disabling of scroll transforms.
- https://nextjs.org/docs/app/guides/static-exports — static output; local preoptimized images rather than a server optimizer.
- https://www.eileenfisher.com/ — concise fabric-led campaign narrative and separation of editorial story from commerce; no layout or assets copied.
- https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Sans+Arabic — readable Arabic display and text from a single coherent family.
- https://unsplash.com/license — licensed placeholder photography, locally hosted with provenance.
